MCA Department



Innovate. Develop. Lead the Digital Future.

The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) at Mohandas College of Engineering and Technology (MCET) is a two-year postgraduate programme affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU), Kerala. The programme is designed to develop highly skilled computing professionals capable of addressing the evolving challenges of the global technology ecosystem.

With a strong emphasis on academic excellence, innovation, and industry relevance, the programme provides an advanced understanding of computer science, software engineering, intelligent computing, cloud technologies, data analytics, and secure computing. Students are equipped with the knowledge and practical competencies required to design, develop, deploy, and manage modern software systems and digital solutions.

Duration

2 years (4 semesters)

Mode

Full time Programme

Eligibility

Graduation in any discipline with minimum 50% marks.

Intake

60 seats

Affiliation

Approved by AICTE & Affiliated to APJKTU.
